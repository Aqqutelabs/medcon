# School microsite content audit

This report records information that was unclear, incomplete, time-sensitive or unsupported in the supplied PLTCI and TMTCS source archives. These items should be confirmed before the pages are treated as definitive admissions advice.

## Applies to both schools

- **Current tuition:** Existing figures are described as indicative. Obtain official 2026/2027 fee schedules showing tuition, compulsory fees, deposits, refunds and payment deadlines.
- **USD conversions:** No single conversion date or approved institutional exchange rate was supplied. Costs are therefore framed as USD planning estimates rather than fixed quotations. use current current conversions and round up to the nearest hundred
- **Flight cost:** Lagos–Manila fares change daily by travel date, airline, baggage and routing. A live return-flight quotation is needed at the point of travel planning. 
- **Nigerian licensing:** A World Directory of Medical Schools listing is not automatic MDCN approval or a licence to practise. Confirm the full degree and internship pathway directly with MDCN before enrolment.
- **JAMB:** JAMB does not replace Philippine entry tests or degree prerequisites. Its precise role in MedCon’s Nigerian screening process needs a documented policy.
- **Visa:** Current Philippine student-visa requirements, authentication steps, fees and processing responsibility should be checked for each admitted student.
- **Loans:** No verified Nigerian education-loan partner, terms or eligibility criteria were supplied.
- **Scholarships:** No current award names, values, quotas or eligibility rules were verified. The pages direct applicants to the official MedCon number instead of publishing an unsupported count.
- **Intakes and calendar:** Confirm application deadlines, teaching dates, holidays, assessment periods and clinical blocks for each cohort.
- **Testimonials:** The supplied archives did not provide enough clearly consented, current and program-specific testimonials for publication.
- **Country comparisons:** Relative-cost descriptions are directional. Comparable annual totals and a shared methodology are still required for an authoritative comparison.
- **Nigeria terminology:** “MBBS Philippines” is retained as a familiar navigation label, while the actual Philippine qualification described is Doctor of Medicine (MD).

## PLTCI College of Medicine

- Confirm the official current name: the source alternates between PLT College, PLTCI and PLTCI College of Medicine.
- Confirm the medical program’s first operating year; the institution’s 1929 founding date is not the medical school’s establishment date.
- Confirm the current chairman, dean, leadership team and approved biographies before publishing named profiles or messages.
- Supply the exact board-approved mission and vision wording.
- Provide documentary details for CHED authority, directory listing, accreditation and any claims of international recognition.
- Confirm all active hospital affiliations, rotation locations, specialties and agreement types.
- Provide the official curriculum by year, credit/unit load, assessment rules, progression policy and internship arrangement.
- Clarify whether PLTCI offers or arranges a distinct pre-med pathway and who is eligible for it.
- Provide an official simulation-lab inventory and current campus-tour captions.
- Verify the “100% English” claim and define the international-student count or proportion.
- Confirm accommodation providers, occupancy options and current monthly prices.
