# School microsite content audit

This report records the remaining unresolved or time-sensitive items after MedCon supplied additional confirmations in August 2026.

## Applies to both schools

- **Tuition changes:** The supplied fee breakdowns are published, but bank-time conversion rates, refunds, deadlines and future changes still require confirmation in each offer letter.
- **USD conversions:** TMTCS peso fees use PHP 56.289 per US$1 from the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas August 2026 table. PLTCI INR source figures use INR 96.2219 per US$1 from the RBI’s 2026 reference display. Results are rounded upward to the nearest US$100 for planning.
- **Flight cost:** The site uses MedCon’s supplied US$1,200 Lagos–Manila allowance. Actual fares remain itinerary-dependent.
- **Nigerian licensing:** MedCon confirms MDCN approval. Individual graduates must still meet the MDCN rules in force when registering.
- **JAMB:** MedCon confirms a minimum score of 200, described as above the 50th percentile, in place of the source site’s Indian NEET reference.
- **Visa:** The Nigerian 9(f) process and document checklist supplied by MedCon are implemented. Embassy requirements can change and should be reconfirmed before interview.
- **Intake:** The September 2026 start is implemented. Exact orientation, teaching, holiday, examination and clinical-block dates remain outstanding.
- **Testimonials:** Current, consented and program-specific student quotations remain unavailable.
- **Country comparisons:** The expanded table uses supplied/source comparisons for available countries and planning extrapolations for the remaining destinations. It is decision guidance, not a fee quotation.
- **Terminology:** “MBBS Philippines” remains a familiar navigation label; the Philippine qualification is Doctor of Medicine.

## PLTCI College of Medicine

- PLTCI source content is treated as confirmed by MedCon, including the five-year pathway, English-medium delivery, PLTCI Medical Center exposure and published facilities.
- Named chairman and dean identities were not supplied, so their messages are summarised by office rather than attributed to an unverified person.
- Exact course units, assessment weighting, progression/remediation rules and dated academic calendar remain unavailable.
- A detailed equipment inventory and captions for each campus photograph remain unavailable.
- Confirm current room occupancy options and whether the published hostel range changes for Nigerian students.

## The Manila Times College of Subic School of Medicine

- The confirmed public name is **The Manila Times College of Subic School of Medicine**.
- Dr Rosalinda Elena B. Milla is presented as Founding Dean and Dr Corazon Valdez as Dean.
- All four source-listed hospital affiliations are treated as current. Specialty-by-hospital rotation allocation remains unavailable.
- The published NMAT minimum remains 40 percentile. Confirm how this interacts with Nigerian JAMB screening for each applicant.
- No detailed MD-specific simulation-lab inventory, academic calendar, examination policy or distinct emergency-medicine module was found.
- The four-year MD structure is used. The separately supplied PHP 120,000 “fifth year (one semester)” fee is displayed as an additional line; its precise academic purpose should still be labelled by the school.
- Current international-student numbers and verified MD student testimonials remain unavailable.
- Partner agreement types, duration and logo-use permissions remain unavailable.

## Implemented confirmations

- Loans and scholarships were removed from both admissions pages.
- The full school-initiated 9(f) visa sequence and Nigerian document checklist were added.
- Both cost pages now contain tuition breakdowns and expanded six-country comparison tables.
- TMTCS fees, PLTCI source costs, the US$1,200 travel allowance and September 2026 intake are reflected on the site.
- Images remain local in `img/school/`; the microsites do not hotlink to source archives.
