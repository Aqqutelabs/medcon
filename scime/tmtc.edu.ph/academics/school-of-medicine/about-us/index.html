About the School of Medicine

About TMTCS School of Medicine

The Manila Times College of Subic, School of Medicine was granted a permit to offer the Doctor of Medicine program to aspiring medical students beginning August 2021. This milestone reflects the institution’s continued commitment to excellence in health sciences education and to the development of future healthcare professionals.

The College has a strong track record of producing competent and compassionate leaders in the medical field. It first introduced the Bachelor of Science in Nursing program in 2008, followed by the Bachelor of Science in Medical Laboratory Science as the nursing program continued to grow. Both programs have consistently achieved high passing rates in the National Board Examinations, with the BS Nursing program attaining a 100 percent passing rate on four occasions.

In response to the growing demand for quality healthcare education, the College further expanded its academic offerings to include programs such as Bachelor of Science in Physical Therapy and Bachelor of Science in Radiologic Technology. Guided by a clear vision to become a leading institution in the training of healthcare providers, the administration established the School of Medicine to strengthen its role in advancing medical education and contributing to the improvement of healthcare in the community.

Mission Statement

The TMTCS School of Medicine is committed to developing competent, ethical, and socially responsive physicians through an outcome based medical education that emphasizes critical thinking, lifelong learning, and safe, high-quality patient-centered care. Through multidisciplinary collaboration, mentoring, and institutional partnerships, the School integrates scientific knowledge, clinical competence, effective communication, and professional values to improve health outcomes and support universal health care.

Vision Statement

The TMTCS School of Medicine envisions itself as a center of innovation in medical education and healthcare, recognized locally and globally for excellence, integrity, and social accountability. The School aims to contribute to the advancement of global health by preparing future-ready physicians and fostering innovation, collaboration, and sustainable health solutions for diverse communities.
