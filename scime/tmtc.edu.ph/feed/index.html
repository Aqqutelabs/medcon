Sankara Consultancy for International Medical Education brings valuable expertise in helping students navigate these important decisions. This partnership ensures that students receive accurate information directly from an authorized representative of the institution.

Creating Opportunities for Students Across South Asia

India, Nepal, and Bangladesh have long produced talented students who aspire to pursue careers in medicine. Many of these students seek educational institutions that combine academic excellence with affordable tuition, quality clinical education, and an internationally recognized curriculum.

The Manila Times College of Subic School of Medicine offers an attractive opportunity for these students by providing a comprehensive medical education in an English speaking academic environment.

Through Sankara Consultancy, students from these countries will have greater access to admissions guidance and a clearer understanding of the opportunities available at The Manila Times College of Subic.

This partnership also helps families make informed decisions by providing reliable information about the admissions process, academic programs, tuition, student life, and living in the Philippines.

Applicants can receive assistance with understanding admissions requirements, preparing application documents, completing application forms, and staying informed about important deadlines.

Once admitted, students will continue working with the institution to prepare for their arrival in the Philippines and begin their journey toward earning a medical degree.

Preparing Future Physicians for a Global Healthcare Environment

Healthcare continues to evolve as medical professionals respond to new challenges, technologies, and patient needs. Medical schools play a vital role in preparing future physicians who possess not only scientific knowledge but also strong communication skills, empathy, and ethical decision making.

The Manila Times College of Subic School of Medicine recognizes the importance of developing well rounded physicians who are prepared to serve diverse communities both locally and internationally.

International partnerships play an increasingly important role in expanding educational opportunities and building stronger academic communities.

Looking Ahead

Together, both organizations are committed to expanding access to quality medical education, strengthening international outreach, and supporting students as they pursue their dreams of becoming physicians.

]]> 3394 TMTC Subic staffer reaps honors for short film https://tmtc.edu.ph/tmtc-subic-staffer-reaps-honors-for-short-film/ Sat, 03 Dec 2022 10:31:38 +0000 https://tmtc.edu.ph/?p=3391 A SHORT film created by a member of The Manila Times College of Subic–Creative Arts & Multimedia Program (TMTCS-CAMMP) has earned accolades from various cinematic award-giving institutions.

“Pagtatapos” by Jhonny Mercida Bobier 2nd, a 22-year-old self-learned filmmaker and director, won the Jury Special Citation from the 34th Gawad Cultural Center of the Philippines’ (CCP) Alternatibo for the experimental category and Best Film in the Region Cinema in Region 14 from the National Center for Culture and the Arts (NCCA) National Committee on Cinema.

The film is a depiction of how the despair, anxiety, harassment and pressure students go through in and outside of school constantly threaten to kill their passion to continue.

“Ang tagumpay ay hindi tungkol sa kung gaano kalakas ang lagapak ng bagsak mo, tungkol ito kung gaano kasakit ang kaya mong tanggapin mula sa mundo at manatili pa ring nakatayo para magpatuloy, ganoon tinatapos ang tagumpay (Success is not measured by how hard you fall. It’s about how you are able to bounce back and continue)!” Bobier said.

The TMTCS-CAMMP aims to provide learning opportunities and experiences in the creative arts and multimedia to young people.

The program teaches students to continuously build and hone their skills to become future leaders in the creative sector.

Dwight Moody E. Bernardo, advertising manager of TMTC Subic, said self-taught youths like Bobier are creating “not just an output you can barely call a movie, but a movie of incredible quality. The writing, plot, direction, execution, and even the editing all feel like they were done by experienced professionals.”
