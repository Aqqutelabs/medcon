Who Can Apply

Eligibility Criteria 2025-26

  • ✓ 12th Pass with Physics, Chemistry, Biology (PCB) — minimum 50% (40% for SC/ST/OBC)
  • ✓ Valid NEET scorecard — mandatory per NMC norms for MBBS abroad
  • ✓ Age — Must be 17 years on or before 31 December of admission year
  • ✓ Valid Indian passport (or applied for — we help with this)
  • ✓ Medical fitness certificate from a registered doctor
NMC Requirement

NEET Score Required

As per NMC (National Medical Commission) regulations, all Indian students applying for MBBS abroad must have a valid NEET qualification:

General Category
50th Percentile
Minimum requirement for MBBS abroad eligibility.

40th Percentile

Common Questions

Admission FAQs

When does the 2025-26 batch start? The 2025-26 academic year typically starts in September-October. Apply early as seats fill fast — limited intake for Indian students.
Is there an entrance exam besides NEET? No. PLTCI does not conduct any additional entrance exam. A valid NEET qualification is sufficient for admission.
Can I apply without a passport? You can start the admission process while applying for your passport. The passport is required before visa application. We help fast-track this.
What is the application fee? Initial counselling and application guidance are completely free. Official PLTCI application charges apply at admission confirmation stage — full transparency on cost before you pay.
