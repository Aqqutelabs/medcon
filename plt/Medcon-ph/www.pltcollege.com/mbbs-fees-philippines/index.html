Living Expenses for Students in Philippines

The Philippines is one of the most affordable destinations for Indian students pursuing MBBS abroad. Monthly living costs at PLTCI are manageable — far lower than Western countries and comparable to studying in Indian metros.

Living expenses in Philippines are generally affordable for international students. Local transportation, internet, shopping, and daily essentials are available at reasonable prices — making it easy for Indian students to manage their monthly budget comfortably.

Most students find Philippines offers a very balanced lifestyle — quality international education combined with affordable day-to-day costs, familiar tropical climate, and an English-speaking environment.

Is Indian Food Available at PLTCI Philippines?

PLTCI College of Medicine ensures Indian students have access to familiar food from Day 1. The dedicated Indian mess on campus serves South and North Indian food three times daily — no compromise needed on food preferences.

Student Facilities

The Indian mess at PLTCI serves both vegetarian and non-vegetarian South and North Indian meals throughout the day. Students who prefer to eat at the campus cafeteria or explore local food will also find affordable options available.

Food expenses vary depending on personal preferences and lifestyle. Using the Indian mess is the most economical and familiar option for most Indian students at PLTCI.
