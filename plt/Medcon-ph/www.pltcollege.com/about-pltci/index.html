Our Story

History & Legacy of PLTCI College of Medicine

PLTCI College of Medicine, Philippines is committed to providing quality medical education for students who dream of becoming successful doctors. The institution focuses on academic excellence, clinical exposure, research-based learning, and international medical standards.

PLTCI College of Medicine

Over the years, PLTCI has continued to support local and international students by creating a student-friendly learning environment with modern facilities, experienced faculty, and practical medical training.

Today, PLTCI College of Medicine is becoming a preferred choice for Indian students looking to study MBBS in Philippines — thanks to its English medium education, affordable fee structure, and strong academic support system.

The college continues to grow with a vision to develop future healthcare professionals who can serve communities with skill, compassion, and professionalism.

  • Modern Medical Curriculum
  • Clinical Hospital Exposure from Year 1
  • International Learning Environment

  • Practical Medical Training with Experienced Faculty

Mission & Vision

PLTCI College of Medicine is guided by a clear mission and a forward-looking vision — to produce competent, compassionate, and globally ready medical professionals.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide quality medical education that helps students build strong academic knowledge, clinical skills, ethical values, and professional responsibility in the field of medicine.

The institution focuses on preparing future doctors through classroom learning, laboratory practice, hospital exposure, and patient-centered medical training.

🔭

Our Vision

To become one of the respected medical institutions in the Philippines by offering globally focused medical education, advanced learning facilities, and strong clinical training for future healthcare professionals.

PLTCI envisions creating doctors who are competent, compassionate, and capable of serving healthcare systems worldwide.

Recognition & Standards

Is PLTCI Recognised by NMC and WHO?

PLTCI College of Medicine follows recognised medical education standards designed to support quality learning and international medical opportunities for students.

🇵🇭

CHED Recognised

Recognised by the Commission on Higher Education, Philippines — the official regulatory body for higher education.

📋

NMC Guidelines Aligned

Curriculum aligned with NMC (National Medical Commission, India) standards to support FMGE / NExT eligibility.

🌍

International Standards

Medical education delivered in English, aligned with international medical education frameworks and global practices.

🏥

Clinical Hospital Exposure

Students receive hospital-based training at PLTCI Medical Center — real patient care from Year 1.

📚

FAIMER & ECFMG

Institution listed with international medical education registries supporting global medical career pathways.

🎓

FMGE / NExT Eligible

Graduates are eligible to appear for India's FMGE and NExT licensing examinations upon returning to India.

ℹ️
Students planning to study MBBS in Philippines should always review the latest eligibility and recognition guidelines applicable in their respective countries before admission. Our counsellors will guide you through the current NMC requirements.
