## **2\. Website Objective**

The MedCon website should present **Medcon Educational Services and Consultancy Limited** as a credible, modern, and parent-trusted medical education admissions company helping African students access medical study opportunities abroad, starting with partner medical colleges in the Philippines.

The website must:

1. Explain what MedCon does.  
2. Educate students and parents about studying medicine in the Philippines.  
3. Showcase partner medical colleges clearly.  
4. Build confidence through parent-focused information, process clarity, and professional design.  
5. Convert visitors into leads through **Apply Now**, **Chat on WhatsApp**, **Parent Consultation**, and **Agent Registration** forms.

This is a **website PRD only**. Do not build dashboards, portals, login systems, document upload systems, admin tools, or app features under this scope.

---

## **3\. Visual Direction**

### **Overall Style**

The website should look like a **premium modern college admissions website**, not a regular consultancy website.

It should combine:

* Professional medical education tone  
* Clean white layouts  
* Deep navy authority sections  
* Blue/teal accent gradients  
* Large confident typography  
* Premium cards  
* Subtle motion design  
* Smooth section transitions  
* High-quality student/campus imagery  
* Strong spacing and visual hierarchy

### **Design Rules**

Use the existing `design.md` system as the foundation.

Follow:

* Deep navy as the primary authority color  
* Blue/teal gradient accents  
* White and light gray backgrounds  
* Soft rounded cards  
* Clean button states  
* Professional SaaS-style form inputs  
* Grid-based layouts  
* Responsive mobile-first spacing  
* Consistent icon style

### **Motion Design Direction**

Modern websites are increasingly using restrained micro-interactions, scroll-triggered reveals, and subtle motion to improve polish without harming usability. Recent web design trend discussions highlight motion, micro-interactions, bold typography, dark sections, accessibility, and performance-focused design as major directions for 2025–2026.

Motion must be **subtle, useful, and controlled**. NN/g’s UX guidance warns that animation should support attention, comprehension, feedback, state change, or navigation—not distract users.

Recommended motion:

* Hero text fades/slides in on page load.  
* CTA buttons have soft hover lift.  
* College cards lift slightly on hover.  
* Stats count up once when visible.  
* Process steps reveal sequentially on scroll.  
* Images use subtle parallax or depth shift.  
* FAQ answers expand smoothly.  
* Form success state animates gently.  
* Floating WhatsApp button pulses lightly, not constantly.

Avoid:

* Excessive parallax  
* Spinning icons  
* Distracting loops  
* Slow page transitions  
* Heavy Lottie animations everywhere  
* Motion that delays access to content

* Use semantic HTML  
* Use accessible forms and labels  
* Ensure keyboard navigation  
* Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
